From raw data to business decisions

Business intelligence (BI) turns millions of rows into actions a manager can take. It uses two tools: OLAP and data mining.

Engine 1 · you ask

OLAP is a flashlight

You already have a question. OLAP lights up the answer fast: totals, summaries, "what happened?"

Engine 2 · it finds

Data mining is a metal detector

You sweep the data. It beeps at patterns you did not know were there: groups, rules, predictions, odd cases.

Support

OLAP = 온라인 분석 처리 (look at data you have). Data mining = 데이터 마이닝 (find hidden patterns).

Part 5 · Ethics · links to A4.4.1 and TOK

Powerful data, real people

Privacy 개인정보

Card data shows where you go and what you buy.

Anonymise data, get consent, keep it secure, and only collect what you need.

Bias 편향

A model learns from past decisions, including unfair ones.

A loan classifier trained on old approvals could keep refusing people from one district or age group.

Transparency 투명성

People affected by a decision deserve a reason.

If a model declines a loan, the bank should explain why, and a named person should be accountable.
