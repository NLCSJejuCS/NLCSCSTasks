        The other two ACID properties, briefly

        Consistency: a transaction moves the database from one valid state to another, so rules such as "balance can't go below 0" or "total money is unchanged by a transfer" are never broken. Durability: once a transaction is committed it survives crashes, because it has been written to permanent storage (and, in a distributed system, usually to more than one node).
